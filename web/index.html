02 · How far it gets

How far a story gets depends on how early it is cut off.

Each step, the people already reaching others pass it to a share of those who haven't seen it. Moderation removes a fraction of that pull. The grey lines are four fixed policies; the red line is yours.

People exposed to the story at each step, for four fixed moderation policies and for the current setting.

03 · Who passes it on

Same story, same odds. A different network, a different outcome.

Each active sharer tries to pass the story to every neighbour who hasn't seen it, and the platform may quarantine each sharer. In a scale-free network a few hubs reach most people; in a random one nobody does.

Reached, active and moderated accounts at each step.

04 · The tipping point

Past one level of moderation, spreading stops paying.

Here the payoffs are not fixed. They are built from the simulation: the sharer's reward grows with β, the platform's damage with how far the story reached, and both pay for moderation. Move m and watch the stable outcome flip.

The sharer's payoff from spreading and from staying quiet as moderation rises from zero to one. The lines cross at the tipping point.
